Nothing leaves your browser: the secret, the QR code and the checks are all computed locally, and the page blocks network requests. The only exception is the optional image URL, which your browser fetches to show the logo.

Follows the Key URI Format, RFC 4226 (HOTP) and RFC 6238 (TOTP). Inspired by the FreeOTP QR generator.
